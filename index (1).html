How this is calculated

Players: daily players in each month = today’s players × (floor + (1 − floor) × (1 − monthly drop)month − 0.5). The floor is the share of today’s audience that stays long term.

Spending per player: today’s Robux ÷ today’s players, changed by the monthly trend you set. Robux spent per month = players × spend per player × 30.

Developer earnings: Robux spent × (1 − Roblox cut) × (1 + Premium payouts) × USD per Robux × (1 − payment fees).

Profit: developer earnings − partner splits − ad spend − fixed costs. After-tax and stake figures come from this profit.

This is a scenario model, not a prediction. Real results depend on updates, events, trends, thumbnails and ad spend. Check the current DevEx rate on Roblox. Premium payouts and any ad revenue are not included in Robux spent, so add them yourself if you know them.
